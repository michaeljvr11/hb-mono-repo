This agreement governs your participation as a vendor on the H&B E-Commerce marketplace. You accept it when you submit a vendor application.

1. What you are selling through

You sell your own goods directly to H&B customers through our Platform. H&B never takes ownership of your inventory — you remain the seller of record for every item you list, and you are responsible for the goods themselves. This is different from H&B's own Procurement Service, a separate service in which H&B buys goods and resells them; see our Terms of Service for how the two differ.

2. Onboarding — what we check, and what we do not

Vendor onboarding is self-declared and is not independently verified. When you apply you give us a business name (required), and optionally a trading name, a registration number, and your country. We do not currently ask for or review any supporting document — no proof of registration, no identity document, no trading licence — before approving a vendor account.

This is a deliberate decision for the current version of the Platform, not an oversight, and we would rather say so than let you or a customer assume a check happens that does not. Nothing on the Platform should be read as H&B having confirmed your legal identity or your right to trade. You confirm that everything you give us is accurate and that you have the legal right to sell the goods you list, and you remain responsible for that being true.

3. Commission

H&B charges a commission on each sale, currently {{ commissionPercent }}% of the item's price — you receive {{ vendorSharePercent }}%.

This rate can change. Commission is held as a dated history rather than a single setting: when a rate changes, the new rate applies from its effective date onwards, and every order line keeps the rate that was in force when that sale happened. We will never retroactively restate what you earned on an order that has already been placed.

4. Getting paid

Your earnings on a sale become eligible for payout once the order is marked delivered and a {{ damageClaimWindowHours }}-hour damage-claim window has passed with no claim against it. Eligible earnings then accrue and are grouped into two-weekly settlement periods, which is what your vendor earnings screen shows you. Cancelled and refunded lines never count towards your earnings.

That is an accounting model, not an automatic bank payment. H&B does not currently run an automated payout: no settlement job exists, and no money moves to your bank account without a person at H&B arranging it. The figures on your earnings screen tell you what you are owed and when it became eligible; actually receiving it is arranged with our team at {{ contact.email }}. We will replace this section with a description of the real mechanism once one exists rather than describe one that does not.

5. Cancellations

If an order is cancelled because of something on your side — you do not have the stock you listed, or you decline to fulfil — that line does not earn you anything. H&B does not currently charge vendors a cancellation fee; no such fee exists on the Platform. If one is ever introduced, we will state the amount here before it applies to you.

6. Fulfilment

You must hand confirmed orders over to H&B's logistics promptly. We have not set a fixed deadline in hours or days, and no automated timer enforces one today — but repeated failure to fulfil orders you have accepted is grounds for suspension under section 8.

7. Your listings

You are responsible for the accuracy of your product listings — description, price, stock levels, and the images you upload, which you must have the right to use. H&B may remove a listing that breaches this agreement or applicable law: without notice where the issue is urgent or unlawful, and with notice otherwise.

8. Approval, suspension and termination

Every application starts as pending. From there H&B may approve or reject it. An approved vendor may later be suspended, and a suspended vendor may be reinstated; a rejected application is final and cannot be reopened into an approved account. These are the only status changes the Platform allows.

Either party may end this agreement with one month's notice. Orders already in flight when notice is given are fulfilled and settled as normal — ending the agreement doesn't cancel them. If your account ends while you are owed money, that money remains owed to you and is paid out on the normal settlement schedule described in section 4.

9. Your customers' information

To fulfil an order you receive what you need to deliver it — the customer's name, delivery address, and the items they ordered. You may use it for that order and nothing else: not marketing, not resale, not building your own list. Our Privacy Policy sets out how H&B handles personal information generally.
