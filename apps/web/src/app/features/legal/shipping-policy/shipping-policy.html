Where we deliver

Anywhere in Namibia, and domestically within South Africa. The shipping fee that applies today is set per delivery route (the country your order ships from and the country it ships to) and currency — it does not currently vary by city or address within a destination country, so a Windhoek address and an address elsewhere in Namibia on the same route are charged the same fee. If delivery outside Windhoek is ever quoted separately, we'll state that here before it applies to your order.

Shipping fee

A shipping fee is shown as a separate line item at checkout, before you pay, and forms part of your order total. The fee is admin-configurable per delivery route (the country your order ships from and the country it ships to) and per currency, and individual products can carry their own fee where a specific item needs a different rate.

How long delivery takes

Delivery typically takes 14–28 days from order confirmation, covering sourcing, cross-border transit, and last-mile delivery. We have not yet published a breakdown by leg of the journey (domestic South Africa, crossing the border, or domestic Namibia) — that level of detail will follow once it's settled.

Cross-border handling

Orders crossing from South Africa into Namibia pass through customs as part of normal delivery. South Africa and Namibia share a customs union (SACU), so goods of South African origin generally cross without import duty. This does not apply to items sourced by our Procurement Service from outside South Africa (for example from Amazon, Shein, or Temu) — those are genuine imports into Namibia and may attract Namibian import duty and VAT.
