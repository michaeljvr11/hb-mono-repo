The short version

Whether your order attracts Namibian import duty depends on where the goods came from, not on which of our services you used to buy them.

  • Goods of South African origin — including everything sold by our Marketplace vendors trading from South Africa, and Procurement Service items we buy for you from a South African retailer — generally cross into Namibia without import duty. South Africa and Namibia are both members of the Southern African Customs Union (SACU), which is a single customs area.
  • Goods sourced from outside South Africa — for example a Procurement Service order we buy for you from Amazon, Shein, or Temu — are a genuine international import into Namibia. They may attract Namibian import duty and VAT, and SACU does not cover them.

We will not tell you that your order crosses the border duty-free as a blanket statement, because for non-South-African-sourced goods that would not be true.

What this means for the price you pay

Your order total today is made up of the item prices plus a single shipping fee (see our Shipping Policy). It does not include any import duty or VAT — we do not currently calculate, quote, or collect duties at checkout, and no duty line appears on your order.

So where duty or VAT is genuinely payable on a Procurement Service item sourced from outside South Africa, that cost is handled separately from the checkout total and passed on to you at what it actually cost us — we do not add a margin to it. If you are ordering something we will source from outside South Africa and you want to know the likely duty before you commit, contact us at {{ contact.email }} or {{ contact.phoneDisplay }} and we will find out before you order rather than surprise you afterwards.
