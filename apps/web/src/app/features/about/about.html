About H&B

Built by South African and Namibian entrepreneurs, H&B set out to close a real gap between the two markets.

Our Story

We grew up loving the products and culture that flow between South Africa and Namibia — but we saw how hard it was for small businesses to reach customers across the border. Informal networks, high logistics costs, trust issues, and no easy digital way to connect.

As young Namibians and South Africans, we knew the demand was there: Namibians want quality SA goods without hassle, and SA SMEs want new markets without the complexity. So we created H&B — a bridge built on care, reliability, and real people helping real people.

H&B runs on two engines: a procurement service that sources and imports what you can't get locally, and a marketplace that connects customers directly with vendors across the corridor.

Our Mission

“To simplify cross-border trade between South Africa and Namibia by creating a structured, digital platform that empowers small businesses to reach customers directly — with transparency, reliability, and genuine care every step of the way.”

We believe in building long-term relationships, not one-off transactions. That's why we handle every import personally, communicate clearly, and treat your order like it's our own.
