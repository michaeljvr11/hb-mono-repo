The H&B Marketplace

The H&B marketplace lets independent vendors across South Africa and Namibia sell their own products directly to you — every listing is vendor-owned stock, not H&B's.

The Procurement Service above is a standing, permanent service alongside the marketplace — not a stand-in for it. For anything not listed on the marketplace, we source it from South Africa (and beyond) and import it into Namibia on your behalf.
